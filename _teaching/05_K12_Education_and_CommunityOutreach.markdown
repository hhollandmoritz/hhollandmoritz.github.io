I love collaborating with K-12 educators to develop lesson plans and engage with young students. This engagement ranges from organizing continuing education workshops about controversial science topics, to K-12 classroom visits, to running citizen science research projects with teachers.

Below are some resources I regularly share with K-12 educators in advance of classroom visits. If you are a K-12 educator and want to work together, please reach out!

**What is Permafrost? (Article)** - Age range: 6th grade+  [https://climatekids.nasa.gov/permafrost/](https://climatekids.nasa.gov/permafrost/)

**Virtual tour of the Permafrost Tunnel in Fox Alaska:**  Age range: 6th-Adult.  [https://virtualice.byrd.osu.edu/permafrost/](https://virtualice.byrd.osu.edu/permafrost/) Virtual field trip underground in Alaska. Shows what permafrost soils look like below ground, and also includes mammoth bones! I recommend pairing this with one of the articles or videos about permafrost if you use it. 

**Earth's permafrost is heating up (Article)**: Age range: 9th grade + [https://www.snexplores.org/article/earths-permafrost-heating-up-melting](https://www.snexplores.org/article/earths-permafrost-heating-up-melting)


**The role of microorganisms in the Methane Cycle (Article)** Age range: 7th grade+
Frontiers for Young Minds (scientific journal articles aimed for kids [https://kids.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/frym.2019.00133](https://kids.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/frym.2019.00133)

**Deep frozen arctic microbes are waking up:** Age range: 9th+, Adult-oriented popular science article. Touches on a lot of the reasons why we may want to study microbes in the arctic. [https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/deep-frozen-arctic-microbes-are-waking-up/](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/deep-frozen-arctic-microbes-are-waking-up/)

**"What happens when permafrost thaws?"** Age range: 8th+ Canadian news segment video, good for media literacy standards. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WaiXYQJUwQw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WaiXYQJUwQw)

**"Microbes in thawing permafrost: the unknown variable in the climate change equation"** Age range: Advanced 11th grade+; This is a scientific article, written for scientists by scientists - typically it's something I might share with a college freshman seminar on the topic - lots of vocabulary challenges and jargon, but also a great example of the professional writing scientists engage in. [Article here](https://www.nature.com/articles/ismej2011163)

**Resources for educators who want to learn more about permafrost:** [List of resources](https://www.geobotany.uaf.edu/nna/library/pubs/NNA-IRPS_Permafrost_Educational_Resources.pdf) I particularly recommend the Reuters link and frozen-ground cartoons.

**Lesson plan for teachers**: Age range: Can be adapted for K-12. Create permafrost in the classroom and study the effect of thaw. [https://www.pbs.org/edens/denali/permawht.htm](https://www.pbs.org/edens/denali/permawht.htm)

### Microbe Resources
**Pondlife Our Tiny Neighbors** - Age range 6th-12th. Three episode series on microbes in ponds and on moss, hosted by the american museum of natural history. The website also includes lesson plans, activities and other resources related to microbiology generally. [https://www.amnh.org/explore/ology/microbiology/pondlife-our-tiny-neighbors](https://www.amnh.org/explore/ology/microbiology/pondlife-our-tiny-neighbors)

**The dirt on decomposers (Video)** Age range: elementary school. Educational video about decomposing microbes in soils.
[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uB61rfeeAsM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uB61rfeeAsM)

**Soil is Alive! (Video)** Age range: elementary school. Video about soil microorganisms [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-J2FErZHuA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-J2FErZHuA)

**The Microworld right under your feet (Video)** Age range: Advanced middle school, high school-adult. This video discusses the extreme diversity and importance of soil microorganisms. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R3XmQgyvDHY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R3XmQgyvDHY)
